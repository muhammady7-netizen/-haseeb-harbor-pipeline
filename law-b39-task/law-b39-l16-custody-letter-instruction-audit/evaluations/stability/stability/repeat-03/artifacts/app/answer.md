# The draft letter weighed against the client's two messages

Letter lines at odds with the record: 197

The review applies the protocol strictly: where the clarification speaks, it governs even if the draft cites an original entry, including tone, behavioural plan, concern, interim contact and without-prejudice settlement tone as later clarified; where the clarification is silent, the original governs even if the draft cites a clarification id for the wrong subject; when a line cites a clarification entry for a different subject, RP-407 sends the line back to the original instruction entry for its own subject, not the clarification; fabricated entry ids and subjects neither message covers are not in the record. Verified lines track the governing entry's position in full, including every required limb; under RP-408 a line that asserts only one limb of a compound position (for example the without-prejudice marking without the soft, conciliatory tone the entry also requires) takes something narrower and is at odds, not verified. At-odds lines reverse a person, keep a superseded soft tone, drop a required limb, address only one aspect of interim contact, use a firm tone on a without-prejudice line, change delivery away from post, copy someone, or otherwise depart from that governing entry.
