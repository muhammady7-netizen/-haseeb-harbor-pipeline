Review of the draft letter against the client's record.

The draft letter was reviewed line by line against the original instruction and the later clarification, applying the protocol that the clarification prevails on any subject it addresses and the original instruction governs only where the clarification leaves the subject alone. Each line was weighed against the governing entry for its subject, not against the cited entry printed in the draft. Where the clarification speaks to a subject, the governing entry is the clarification entry; where neither record speaks to a subject, the line is marked NOT_IN_RECORD.

The review table records, for each numbered line, whether the client's record verifies it, whether it is at odds with the record, or whether the record never speaks to it. The table also names the record entry that governs each line. Lines that take the full position of the governing entry are VERIFIED; lines that take a narrower, reversed, or different position are AT_ODDS. Lines on subjects outside both messages are NOT_IN_RECORD, regardless of any citation in the draft.
