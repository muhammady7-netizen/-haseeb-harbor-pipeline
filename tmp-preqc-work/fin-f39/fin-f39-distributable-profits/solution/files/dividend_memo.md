# The proposed final dividend

Profits available for distribution are **2,625,000**. Interim dividends of
**750,000** have already been **paid** since the last annual accounts, so the maximum
further distribution is **1,875,000**. A third interim of 200,000 has been
**declared but not yet paid**; a fourth interim of 100,000 has status **PAID** which
is not the same as **Paid** — the policy says only the exact token `Paid` (case-sensitive)
counts as paid. Neither reduces the profits available for a further distribution. The final
dividend the board has proposed is **1,420,000**, which is within the maximum, so
**it may lawfully be paid**.

## Revaluation surpluses realised on disposal

**Revaluation reserve A** holds 900,000: 580,000 for Willowbrook House (still held,
unrealised) and 320,000 for Fairhurst Road (disposed in October, realised on disposal).
The 320,000 is available for distribution whether or not it has been transferred out of
the reserve — and it has not been transferred.

**Revaluation reserve B** holds 600,000 across three properties:
- 200,000 for Treetop Apartments (still held, unrealised) — not available.
- 300,000 for Maple Court (fully disposed in November, realised on disposal) — available.
- 100,000 for Oak Plaza, of which 50,000 relates to the disposed 50% (realised) and
  50,000 relates to the retained 50% (still held, unrealised).

Only the disposed portions are realised: 320,000 + 300,000 + 50,000 = 670,000.
The unrealised portions (580,000 + 200,000 + 50,000 = 830,000) join the non-distributable
capital.

## Fair value gains, treated by what the asset is

All gains were credited to profit or loss and sit inside retained earnings. The test is
what the asset is, not where the gain was booked.

The gain on **listed equity investments** (95,000) is **realised**: quoted, small holding,
sold at quoted price without negotiation. **No adjustment**.

The gain on **UK government bonds** (60,000) is **realised**: gilts have a deep market,
can be sold at the screen price without negotiation, and a sale would not affect the
price. The bonds are readily convertible to cash. **No adjustment**.

The gain on **Brookvale investment property** (180,000) is **unrealised**: valuer's
opinion, marketing and negotiation needed. **Deducted** in full.

The gain on **Helmsley private equity** (85,000) is **unrealised**: unquoted, no
reliable price, negotiation needed. **Deducted** in full.

## Development costs — two projects, different treatment

**Software project A** (260,000): carried as an intangible asset. The finance director's
email is NOT notes to the accounts. Special circumstances must be stated in the notes,
and they have not been. The whole amount is a **realised loss** and is **deducted**.

**Software project B** (120,000): carried as an intangible asset. The directors HAVE
justified special circumstances in **Note 17 of the notes to the accounts**, stating
future economic benefits and recognition conditions. The exception applies and this
amount is **NOT a realised loss**. **No adjustment**. Treating both projects the same
would overstate the deduction by 120,000.

## Provisions — two different treatments

The **Denby litigation provision** (140,000) was charged in accordance with accounting
standards. It is a **realised loss** already reflected in retained earnings. It is not
added back. **No adjustment**.

The **Elmwood restructuring provision** (80,000) was charged to profit or loss but was
NOT measured in accordance with accounting standards — it does not follow IAS 37. The
policy says only a provision "properly charged in accordance with accounting standards"
is a realised loss. This provision is not, so it is **not a realised loss** and must be
**added back** to retained earnings.

## The fourth interim — PAID is not Paid

The fourth interim dividend of 100,000 has status **PAID** in the record. The policy
says "only the exact token `Paid` (case-sensitive) counts as paid." **PAID** is not
**Paid**. It does not count as a distribution already made and does not reduce the
profits available for a further distribution. The second interim's status carries a
leading space; after trim it is `Paid` and correctly counts.

## Conclusion for the board

The two paid interims of 750,000 stand. The proposed final dividend of 1,420,000
**may lawfully be paid** in full: it does not exceed the 1,875,000 still available.
The unpaid third interim and the case-insensitive fourth interim do not reduce the
pool.
