# Review of the draft letter against the client's record

The draft was checked line by line against the client's first message and later clarification. Under the review protocol, the clarification prevails on every subject it speaks to, the original instruction governs only the subjects the clarification leaves alone, and subjects neither message addresses have no governing record entry. Each line was therefore weighed against the entry that governs its subject, not against the entry the draft happens to cite.

Letter lines at odds with the record: 155

Of the 290 draft lines, 86 are verified by the governing record entry, 155 are at odds with it, and 49 are not in the record at all because neither the original instruction nor the clarification speaks to their subject. 168 lines are governed by a clarification entry rather than an original instruction entry. The most common failures are lines that cite an original entry after the clarification has superseded it, lines that give only part of a required position, and lines that address the father or his attorney when the clarification directs the letter to the mother's attorney.
